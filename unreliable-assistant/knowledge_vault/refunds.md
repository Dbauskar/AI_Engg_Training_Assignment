# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. The enterprise licence SKU-099 is non-refundable once it has been activated. Any refund above 5,000 USD requires approval from a support manager before it is issued. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 business days.
