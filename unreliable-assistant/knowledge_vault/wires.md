# Wire Transfer Approval Policy

Every wire transfer above 10,000 USD requires approval from two authorised approvers. Transfers above 250,000 USD additionally require sign-off from the compliance officer. The requester can never approve their own transfer, and no system or software agent may execute a transfer without a recorded human approval.
