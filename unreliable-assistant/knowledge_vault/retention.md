# Data Retention Policy

Customer records are retained for 7 years after account closure. Application and audit logs are retained for 13 months, after which they are deleted or irreversibly anonymised. Data subject deletion requests must be completed within 30 days of a verified request. Legal holds override all retention schedules until released by the Legal department.
