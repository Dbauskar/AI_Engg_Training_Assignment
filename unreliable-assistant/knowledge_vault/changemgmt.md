# Change Management Policy

Every production change requires an approved ticket from the Change Advisory Board and a tested rollback plan. A change freeze applies from 15 December to 3 January, during which only emergency fixes approved by the VP of Engineering may be deployed. Emergency changes must be reviewed retrospectively within 2 business days.
