# Hardware Warranty Policy

Standard hardware carries a 24 month warranty from the delivery date. The battery pack SKU-205 is covered for 12 months only. Damage caused by liquid ingress or unauthorised repair voids the warranty. Replacement units are shipped within 3 business days of an approved claim.
