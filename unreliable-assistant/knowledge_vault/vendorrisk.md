# Vendor Risk Management

Tier 1 vendors, those handling customer data or critical services, must be assessed every year and must hold a current SOC 2 Type II report. A signed data processing agreement is required before any personal data is shared. Vendors that fail assessment are placed on a remediation plan with a 90 day deadline.
